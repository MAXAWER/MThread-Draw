# Where the sample images come from

Every photograph here is **CC0 / public domain**, which is the only licence that
leaves both halves of this project's own licensing clean: a file under CC BY-SA
would follow anyone who takes a commercial licence, and attribution obligations
are a poor thing to hide in an examples folder. The credits below are given
because it is decent, not because the licence demands them.

All four were resized to 1400 pixels on the long edge and re-encoded; nothing
else was changed, and in particular nothing was retouched, masked or prepared
for tracing. That is the point of them.

They were chosen after tracing about sixty candidates and looking at the
results. The subjects that work are the ones with a readable silhouette; the
ones that fail are flat grey structure against a flat grey background, where
every line is real and none of them tells you what you are looking at.

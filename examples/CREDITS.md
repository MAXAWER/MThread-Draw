# Where the sample images come from

## launchpad.jpg

Space Shuttle Endeavour on Launch Pad 39A, February 2010. NASA, ID
201002060005HQ, from <https://images.nasa.gov>.

NASA still images are generally not protected by copyright and may be used for
any purpose, subject to NASA media usage guidelines - notably that NASA
insignia may not be used, and that NASA does not endorse anything they appear
in. The photograph shows no identifiable people.

It is here because a demo needs a real photograph with real detail: a launch
tower is several hundred strokes of lattice that no illustration would fairly
stand in for.
